About

Biography

I am a petrologist and geochemist who uses forward geochemical models to address questions about Earth’s tectonics and planet formation. I use chemical diffusion models to constrain the timescales of metamorphic processes and investigate if and how they have changed over Earth’s history. My current work uses phase-equilibrium models to predict the compositions of condensed solid materials in the protoplanetary disks. Besides modeling, I also employ a variety of field and analytical techniques to decipher the rock record.

I am a postdoctoral fellow in Earth and Environmental Sciences and Astronomy at the University of Michigan, working with Lia Corrales, Robert Holder, and Jie Li.

Under review · Precambrian Research

Rapid metamorphic cooling of the Quetico Subprovince, Canada, challenges the paradigm of long-lived slow-cooling Archean metamorphism

Xu, Y., Holder, R. M., Hames, W. E. (2026)

We investigate the metamorphic cooling history of the Neoarchean Quetico Subprovince of the Superior Province using 40Ar/39Ar thermochronology and garnet diffusion modeling. Our findings challenge the assumption that slow-cooling Archean metamorphism reflects a tectonic regime fundamentally different from modern plate tectonics. Currently available timescale data are limited in temporal resolution and it is possible that rapid cooling of orogenic belts could have been more prevalent in the Archean than often considered.

In preparation

Rapid cycling and exhumation of metamorphic rocks in a subduction channel: evidence from diffusion modeling on the subduction mélange of Cedros Island, Mexico

Xu, Y., et al.

We use phase-equilibrium and diffusion models on the garnet-amphibolite blocks from the subduction mélange of Cedros Island, Mexico, to reconstruct the P–T–t history of the blocks based on the chemical zoning in garnet. The rocks record rapid cycles in pressure, suggesting convoluted flow of materials in the subduction channel. The sharp chemical gradients in garnet require fast cooling (and exhumation) rates at or above plate velocities.
